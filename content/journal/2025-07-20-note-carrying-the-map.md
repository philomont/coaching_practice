excerpt: A short observation about the one job description line nobody believes — and why it matters more than the title on the door.
---

Every leader I've worked with has at some point been handed some
version of the same line: *"and other duties as assigned."*
Nobody believes it, and yet it's the most accurate line in the
document. The job you actually do is never the one that was
posted.

What matters is noticing which unassigned duties you've
collected — and whether you chose them or absorbed them.
