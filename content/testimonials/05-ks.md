Phil has been my indirect manager twice throughout my career, and I can’t speak highly enough of his leadership style, professionalism, kindness, and generosity. Early in my career, he made a point of involving me in challenging and engaging projects while providing thoughtful, constructive feedback that helped me grow. He also consistently took the time to understand what I found interesting and meaningful in my work, and made a consistent effort to create opportunities for me to pursue those interests. Later, I had the opportunity to learn from him in a different capacity, particularly in how to build trust effectively, navigate important issues with respect and conviction, and foster team alignment and buy-in.

I’ve always admired Phil’s ability to break down complex topics into approachable concepts and create an environment where everyone feels comfortable asking questions. Beyond that, he assures everyone’s voice is heard and valued. He is an exceptional leader and mentor, and I feel fortunate to have had the opportunity to work with him.
