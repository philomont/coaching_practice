I came into my first VP role at 31, technically qualified and
privately sure I was about to be found out. Phil mentored me
through that first year — two honest hours a month, plus the
messages in between when something was happening in real time.

What sets him apart: he listens for what actually matters to
you, not what the textbook says should matter. Then he helps you
turn that into concrete steps — what to say to whom, what to try
this week, and how to tell the difference between fear and
information.

Two years later I lead a larger organization than I once
imagined, and the practices we built together are still the
backbone of how I work. I recommend him to every leader moving
into a bigger seat.
