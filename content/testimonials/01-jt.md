excerpt: Phil didn’t hand me a script. We worked through my goals in my own words, and six months later I’m in a role I helped design.
---

Phil was my manager for three years, and when the department
reorganized in 2023 he made space for me to figure out what I
actually wanted next. Not the obvious next rung, but what I
would still be glad to be doing in five years.

We worked through that in a series of honest conversations: what
my values were, what I was tired of carrying, which parts of my
role made time disappear for me. Then we built a plan around
those answers and iterated on it as the org shifted around us,
revising it a few times where the first version turned out wrong.

I ended up in a role that didn’t exist before that year, one I
helped design, reporting to someone else entirely. The best part:
by the end, the next steps were things I could plot myself. He
worked himself out of a job, which I suspect is the point.
