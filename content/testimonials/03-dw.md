I have known Phil for over 13 years since we worked in the same office in government and we were individual contributors on the same team. He joined about a year after I started and was by far the most collaborative colleague I had, among both federal and contractor staff. He soon became a trusted sounding board to bounce all my ideas and strategies on how to solve all kinds of problems for data, analysis, and work. After a few years we both went onto other paths but have kept in touch with Phil as one of my most valued friends. I periodically asked him for his opinion on my career choices throughout the years and he has even helped connect me to my current position. Of the people I know, he has been one of the few I can freely discuss my career worries with, satisfyingly analyze the situation, and actually come away with getting closer to finding an answer or way forward. I can’t think of a better person to have in the profession of coaching.
