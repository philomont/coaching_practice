I had the privilege of working under Phil at Clarify Health, where he led the Data Science organization. He was my manager and a mentor, and he shaped how I think about building ML systems and about leadership.

Phil had the rare mix of strategic vision and deep technical skill. He set clear direction for the teams, found the problems that would matter most to the business, and scoped them so his teams could deliver. He still led by example. He was never too senior to work through a hard technical problem himself.

His biggest mark on the company was transforming its ML foundation. Phil designed a novel algorithm and championed replacing the entire ML stack with it. He worked through difficult stakeholders across the business to get buy-in, then led the rollout. The result was large cost savings and much better predictions across the company's products.

He also made data science useful well beyond his own team. He had a talent for seeing patterns and building infrastructure that non-technical colleagues could use with confidence. The reporting stack he built was so intuitive that the whole company adopted it.

As a people leader, Phil built talent on purpose. He saw what each person was best at and pushed them to double down on it. When I proposed building a metadata store, he gave me room to experiment, added input at the right moments, and helped turn a rough idea into something exceptional. He grew people by trusting them with real ownership.

Phil raised the bar for his team and for the whole organization. He would be an outstanding leader for any company, and I'd welcome the chance to work with him again.
