For twenty years I’ve worked alongside
leaders in seasons of change — the promotions that outgrew them,
the pivots that looked reckless from the outside, the quiet
question of whether the current path was still the right one.
What I’ve learned is that the best work isn’t scripted.
It’s a close, honest conversation that unfolds over time.

Today I work one-on-one with leaders and professionals:
understanding your values, naming your goals in your own words, and
iterating with you on concrete steps that bring your leadership
objectives to life — using the resources you actually have, not
a fantasy version of them.
