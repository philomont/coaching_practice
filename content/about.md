For approximately twenty years I’ve worked alongside
leaders in the ebb and flow of change. I have witnessed the impact
of re-orgs, promotions of unprepared people into leadership roles,
and leadership styles that were an attempt to copy a perception of
past success, but were ineffective in specific organizations and
situations. I have also witnessed and received excellent mentoring
and leadership. My professional success happened because of
compassionate coaches. What I’ve learned is that the best coaching
happens with one-to-one engagement and an investment of time.

Today I work with leaders and professionals:
understanding your values, structuring your goals, and
iterating with you on verifiable steps that bring your leadership
objectives to life. This pragmatic approach uses the resources
at your disposal.

I came to coaching from the physical sciences, and from my work in data science,
and that background shapes how I work. I’m comfortable with evidence, models, and the
discipline of changing one variable at a time, so the practice is
practical, and based on research and evidence.
Leaders in technical organizations tend to recognize the kind of
rigor they already trust, pointed at decisions and people instead
of systems and data. That said, you don’t have to be a leader in a
technical organization to find value in this approach.

A former boss once described my greatest strength plainly: coaching
extremely technical people. That is the work I have done best across
my organizations, and it matters more now than ever. As AI
accelerates organizations and lifts deeply technical people into
leadership, more leaders arrive in their seats the way I did:
expert in the technology, still growing into the leadership. That is
exactly the coaching I do best.

Alongside leadership coaching, I’ve led professional teams of my own, and
I’ve spent years on the water coaching paddling teams. Helping people
achieve their goals has been one of life’s most rewarding experiences, and my hope is
that you find leadership to be the same for you.
