About Phil

a partner for the leadership work that matters to you

For twenty years I’ve worked alongside leaders in seasons of change — the promotions that outgrew them, the pivots that looked reckless from the outside, the quiet question of whether the current path was still the right one. What I’ve learned is that the best work isn’t scripted. It’s a close, honest conversation that unfolds over time.

Today I work one-on-one with leaders and professionals: understanding your values, naming your goals in your own words, and iterating with you on concrete steps that bring your leadership objectives to life — using the resources you actually have, not a fantasy version of them.

  • 20+ years leading teams through organizational change
  • Certified executive & transition coach
  • 300+ leaders supported through their next chapter

How We Work Together

collaborative by design — and flexible as things change

1

Understand what matters

We start with you — your values, your goals, the context you’re actually in. No intake script, just a real conversation about what’s true and what’s calling.

2

Shape the direction

We turn that into a direction you believe in, with milestones we can measure and adjust. It’s your plan, in your own words.

3

Iterate together

We work in cycles — trying steps, learning from what happens, and course-correcting together. The plan stays alive rather than fixed.

4

Make it real

We ground the work in the resources and realities you have, so the changes stick — and you keep the skills long after we’re done.

Ways to Work Together

shaped around you — not a template

Multi-Session Packages

A focused run of sessions to work through a transition or decision together — with a written recap after each and a plan we refine as we go.

Monthly Partnership

Ongoing sessions with messaging in between, for the leadership work that deserves steady company — kept close and iterative as things evolve.

Long-Term Retainer

Advisory-level partnership for executives in full-scale change — new mandates, restructures, reinventions — where we work alongside you and your team, adapting as the situation evolves.

Every engagement is shaped around you — your goals, your calendar, and the resources you have. Details and pricing come up naturally on our discovery call, and scheduling is handled through my online calendar.
