Base Camp

where we shake hands and check your gear

Before I was a coach, I was the person in the middle of the transition — the promotion that felt like a trapdoor, the career pivot everyone said was reckless, the quiet question “is this really it?” What I learned is that nobody summits alone, and nobody has to.

I coach leaders and professionals through seasons of change: stepping into bigger roles, stepping out of old ones, or stepping toward something they can’t quite name yet. My work is warm, candid, and practical — part thinking partner, part trail guide, full-time in your corner.

Way-station 02

The Route

how a typical passage unfolds

  1. Switchback 1

    Find your footing

    We start with where you actually are — not the polished version, the true one. A deep first session to map the terrain: what’s working, what’s weathered, what’s calling.

  2. Switchback 2

    Chart the passage

    Together we set a bearing: the summit you’re aiming for and the honest switchbacks that will get you there. Real goals, real timelines, real accountability.

  3. Switchback 3

    Climb together

    Regular sessions as we ascend — celebrating the ridgelines, working the scrambles, adjusting the route when the weather changes. Because it will.

  4. Switchback 4

    Stand on the summit

    You arrive — clearer, steadier, more yourself. And you leave with the mountain sense to make the next climb your own.
