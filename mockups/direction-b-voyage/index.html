Waypoint 01

Charting the Course

who’s holding the map, and why

Every meaningful chapter of my own life began the same way: standing at a rail, looking at open water, half thrilled and half certain I’d lost my mind. The career leap. The role that outgrew me. The reinvention nobody approved of. That’s when I learned the difference between drifting and sailing — drifting is what happens to you; sailing is what you choose.

Today I coach leaders and professionals through their passages: executive transitions, career pivots, the season when the old heading stops feeling true. I bring warmth, candor, and two decades of my own weather to every conversation.

  • 20+ years navigating organizational change
  • Certified executive & transition coach
  • 300+ passages guided

Waypoint 02

The Passage

how we sail, from casting off to making landfall

Cast Off

Find true north

An unhurried first session to take an honest sounding: where you are, what you’re carrying, and which horizon keeps catching your eye.

Set Sail

Agree on a heading

We chart the course together — a bearing you believe in, waypoints you can measure, and the honest acknowledgment of where the squalls will be.

Open Water

Make way together

Regular sessions as the passage unfolds. We trim sails, work the weather, and keep the conversation warm even when the water isn’t.

Landfall

Arrive — changed

You reach the far shore steadier, clearer, more yourself. And you keep the seamanship: the ability to make this kind of passage again, on your own.
